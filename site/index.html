judge-jev

Requirement-by-requirement verdicts on whether a repository's implementation meets its records.

For every requirement record (REQ, NFR, ADR) and every place that implements it, judge-jev reports whether that place meets the requirement and, when it does not, why not. The output is one verdict per (record, location) pair.
